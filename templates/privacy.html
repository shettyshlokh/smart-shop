Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Smart Shop ("we", "the app") is made by {{ developer }}. It lets people order from local shops for home delivery or pack & pickup. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, optional email address and password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form).
  • Delivery details: the address you type for delivery.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location" or search an area – used to show nearby shops, distance, delivery area and delivery time. Shop owners save their shop's location.
  • Orders: items, amounts, delivery/pickup choice, status and times.
  • Payments: payment method, payment reference numbers (UPI reference / UTR, Razorpay payment ID) and wallet transactions. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank password – card and netbanking payments are handled by Razorpay, and UPI payments by your own UPI app.
  • Shop owners: shop name, address, phone, email, opening hours, UPI ID and item list.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, take and deliver your orders and show order status and delivery time.
  • To process payments, wallet balance and refunds.
  • To send order updates by SMS, WhatsApp and email.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • The shop you order from sees your name, phone number, delivery address and order so it can prepare and deliver it.
  • Service providers that work for us: Razorpay (payments), Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp), our email provider, our hosting provider, and OpenStreetMap (map tiles and area search, loaded by your device).
  • When required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, saved address, location and wallet history. Shops keep the order amounts for their sales records, but with your name, phone, email and address removed.

5. Your choices

  • Location is optional – you can always type an area instead.
  • You can update your address at checkout and clear your saved location from the home page.
  • Delete your account any time: in the app go to Account → Delete my account, or visit {{ url_for('delete_account_info', _external=True) }}.

6. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, the site uses HTTPS, and online payments are verified with the payment provider before an order is confirmed.

7. Children

Smart Shop is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
