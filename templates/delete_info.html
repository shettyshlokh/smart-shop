Delete your Smart Shop account

You can delete your Smart Shop account and personal data at any time.

Customers

  1. Log in with your phone/email and password.
  2. Open Account → Delete my account (or go straight to this page).
  3. Enter your password, tick the box and press Delete my account.

Shop owners

  1. Log in to your shop.
  2. Open Dashboard → Shop settings → Delete shop account (or this page).

What is deleted

  • Customers: name, phone, email, password, saved address and location, wallet balance and wallet history. Past orders stay in the shop's sales records with your personal details removed.
  • Shops: the shop profile, items, UPI ID, location and its order records.

Deletion happens immediately. Can't log in? Email {{ contact }} from your registered email or tell us your registered phone number, and we will delete it within 30 days.
